User avatar — image or auto-generated initials (handles CJK names), with optional presence ring/dot.

```jsx
<Avatar name="momo" />
<Avatar src="/me.jpg" name="祐祐" size="lg" ring status />
<Avatar name="DK" tone="gold" shape="rounded" size="xl" />
```

- `size`: `xs`–`xl` or a number (px). `shape` `circle`/`rounded`.
- No `src` → initials on a sage/gold/rose tint. `ring` for presence, `status` for the corner dot.
