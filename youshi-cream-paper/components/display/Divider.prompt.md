Separator rules. The `dashed` variant is the brand's signature journal connector.

```jsx
<Divider />
<Divider variant="dashed" tone="sage" />
<Divider orientation="vertical" />
<Divider label="今日手账" />
```

- `orientation` horizontal/vertical; `variant` solid/dashed; `tone` default/sage.
- `label` renders a handwritten caption between two dashed rules — use for warm section breaks.
